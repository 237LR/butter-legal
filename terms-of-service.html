2. Description of Service

Butter is a weekly dinner planner for people who work all day. It uses artificial intelligence (specifically, Grok by xAI) to plan a set of dinners for the nights your household cooks, based on the settings you choose: who eats with you, your cooking nights and dinner time, your diet and ingredients to avoid, your kitchen equipment, your budget, your store and country, and the staples you always have. Your household is described to the AI by counts and children's ages, never by name. The App provides:

  • A week of dinners planned as a set: five, or one for each cooking night your household picks
  • Portions that follow who is home each night
  • One shopping list for the week, sorted by aisle
  • A cooking screen that runs timers in parallel and says what to do next
  • Swaps for any night, with alternatives that reuse what you already bought
  • Optional simple breakfasts and next-day lunchboxes
  • Sharing one week and one shopping list across the phones in your household
