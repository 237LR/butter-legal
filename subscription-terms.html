Subscription Terms

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

This document outlines the plans, pricing, billing, and cancellation policies for Butter+, the optional auto-renewing subscription that lets your household plan new weeks of dinners and swap any night after its free first week. By subscribing to Butter+, you agree to these terms in addition to our Terms of Service.

3. One Subscription per Household

One Butter+ subscription covers everyone in a household. If anyone in your household has an active Butter+ subscription, the whole household has Butter+: everyone can plan new weeks and swap nights from their own phone without buying a subscription of their own.

  • A household is the group of phones that share one week and one shopping list in Butter. People join a household with a code from someone already in it.
  • Only the person who subscribed is billed, through their Apple ID.
  • Butter+ stays with the person who subscribed. If they leave the household, or their subscription ends, the rest of the household no longer has Butter+ unless someone else in it has an active subscription.

4. Fair Use

Butter+ lets your household plan new weeks and swap nights as often as everyday cooking needs, within a fair-use limit of three new weeks and thirty swaps per household per day. If your household reaches the limit, Butter will ask you to try again the next day. The limit exists to prevent automated or abusive use and is not expected to affect normal use.

5. Billing

  • All subscriptions are billed through Apple's App Store and processed via your Apple ID.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Prices are displayed in your local currency. Prices in currencies other than USD may vary due to exchange rates and local taxes.
  • We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information.

6. Auto-Renewal

All Butter+ subscriptions automatically renew unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Your Apple ID account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period at the rate of your selected plan.
  • Monthly plans renew every month on the same date you originally subscribed.
  • Yearly plans renew every 12 months on the same date you originally subscribed.

7. How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you will continue to have access to Butter+ features until then. After that, your household has Butter+ only if someone else in it has an active subscription.

To cancel your subscription:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name (Apple ID) at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Find and tap Butter+.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.

Alternatively, you can manage subscriptions through the App Store app or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. While your subscription is active, Manage on the Butter+ card in the app's Profile tab opens the same page.

Note: Deleting the Butter app or leaving a household does not cancel your subscription. You must cancel through your Apple ID settings to stop future charges.

8. Refunds

Since all purchases are processed through Apple, refund requests must be submitted directly to Apple. To request a refund:

  • Visit reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Sign in with your Apple ID
  • Find the Butter+ charge and select "Request a refund"

Refund eligibility and approval are at Apple's sole discretion, in accordance with Apple's refund policies. We are unable to process refunds directly.

9. Free Trials and Introductory Offers

Butter+ currently has no free trial or introductory offer; instead, each household's first week is free without a subscription (see Section 1). We may introduce free trials or introductory prices in the future. If we do:

  • The offer, its duration, and the price that follows it will be clearly stated before you subscribe.
  • You will not be charged during a free trial.
  • At the end of the offer, your subscription will automatically renew at the standard price of your plan unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the offer ends.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial will be forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

10. Changes to Pricing

The prices in Section 1 apply from September 30, 2026. If you subscribed before that date, you keep your price while your subscription continues.

We reserve the right to change subscription pricing at any time. If we change pricing:

  • Existing subscribers will be notified in advance of any price changes.
  • Price changes will take effect at the start of your next billing period after the change.
  • Apple may notify you of price changes and require you to agree to the new price before your subscription renews.
  • If you do not agree to a price change, you may cancel your subscription before the new price takes effect.

11. Downgrading

If your Butter+ subscription is cancelled or expires, and no one else in your household has an active subscription, your household returns to the free tier at the end of the current billing period. Everything already planned stays usable: the week and its recipes, the shopping list, the cooking screen, and sharing. Planning a new week or swapping a night will need Butter+ again.
