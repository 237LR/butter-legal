Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

Karol Jaworski ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Butter mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. Please read this policy carefully. By using Butter, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Butter has no accounts and no sign-in. Most of what you tell it stays on your phone. What leaves it, and why, is listed below.

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Your household: how many adults and children eat with you, children's ages, and, if you choose, first names for yourself and the people in your household.
  • Food preferences: your diet (for example vegetarian or gluten-free), ingredients you never want, and the staples you always have.
  • Your routine: which nights you cook, when dinner is, whether you want breakfasts or lunchboxes planned, your budget, and your store and country.
  • Your kitchen: how many hobs you have and whether you have an oven, a microwave or an air fryer.

1.2 Information Created When You Use the App

  • Device verification: a random ID created for this installation, and a key ID from Apple's App Attest service. They let our server confirm that requests come from the genuine Butter app on a real device, prevent abuse, and keep the free week and free swaps fair. They are not advertising identifiers and are never used to track you.
  • Usage counts: how many weeks your household has planned and how many dinners it has swapped, so the free allowance and fair use of Butter+ can be applied.
  • Subscription status: if you subscribe to Butter+, the date your subscription runs out, as the App Store reports it, so the subscription covers everyone in your household.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data
  • Health data from the Health app
  • Payment details, which Apple handles entirely
  • Analytics, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind

2. How We Use Your Information

Purpose Data Used
Plan your week of dinners, breakfasts and lunchboxes Household counts and children's ages, food preferences, routine, kitchen, budget, store and country, recent dinner titles
Share one week and one shopping list across your household (only if you invite someone or join a household) Household settings including the first names you entered, the planned week and its recipes, the shopping list's ticks
Verify the app, prevent abuse and apply the free allowance Installation ID, App Attest key ID, usage counts
Give your household Butter+ Subscription expiry date

3. AI-Generated Content & Data Processing

Butter uses Grok, an AI model developed by xAI, to plan dinners and breakfasts. When your week is planned or a dinner is swapped, your household as counts and children's ages, your food preferences, routine, kitchen, budget, store and country are sent through our server to xAI to write the recipes. This data is used only to plan your meals and is subject to xAI's Privacy Policy.

Important: Names are never sent to the AI. The planner hears "two adults and a child aged 5", not who they are.

4. Third-Party Services

Butter relies on the following services:

  • Supabase: hosts Butter's server functions and the database for device verification and shared households. See Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • xAI (Grok): writes the planned meals. See xAI Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: App Store purchases and subscriptions, and App Attest device verification. See Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • On your phone: your settings, the planned week and the shopping list are stored on your device.
  • On our server: the device verification record and usage counts for every installation; and, for shared households only, the household's settings, week and shopping list, and the name each person gave themselves.
  • Security: all traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS). Server data is not reachable with the app's public key: only Butter's server functions can read or write it, and every request must come from a verified device.

6. Data Retention

Data on your phone stays until you delete the app. A shared household's data stays on our server while anyone is in the household; when the last person leaves, the household and everything it shared are deleted. Device verification records and usage counts are kept so the free allowance stays fair. You can ask us to delete any of it at any time (see Section 12).

7. Your Rights

7.1 All Users

  • Access: ask for a copy of the data our server holds about your installation or household.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete it. Leaving a shared household in the app stops your phone sharing; if you were the last member, the household's data is deleted.
  • Correction: change your household, preferences and name in the app at any time.
  • Minimal use: Butter works without names; they are optional.

7.2 European Economic Area (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to data portability, the right to restrict processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Our legal basis for processing is the performance of our service to you (planning and sharing your meals) and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of the service.

7.3 California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your data, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Butter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Parents may enter a child's first name and age so portions and dinners suit the household; this stays within the household's data, and only the age, never the name, is used to plan meals. If you believe we hold data you want removed, contact us at karoljaworsky@gmail.com.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the services listed in Section 4, solely for the purposes described in this policy, and within your household when you choose to share it. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own when using the services above (for example Supabase and xAI). These services maintain appropriate data protection safeguards in compliance with applicable regulations.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and, where appropriate, through an in-app notification. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
